# stx-telemetry

## 0.3.0

### Minor Changes

- [#262](https://github.com/softistx/nxgt-krepo/pull/262) [`e7a0481`](https://github.com/softistx/nxgt-krepo/commit/e7a0481579ce8626699bd8091dda570c4741fc9f) Thanks [@SteveGT96](https://github.com/SteveGT96)! - The export pipeline now survives an exporter that cancels itself and reports a backlog dropped at the drain timeout, `withAttributes` falls back to the installed telemetry, `FileExporter` keeps writing after a failed roll, and `MongoExporter` updates its TTL index in place (`collMod`) and drops it when retention is null or under a second (`collMod` needs the `collMod` privilege, which `readWrite` lacks, so without it the index is dropped and created again; a null retention costs one `listIndexes` at the first batch), a retention step that fails no longer stops the batch from being inserted, and a `FileExporter` whose reopen failed after a roll retries it on the next write.

### Patch Changes

- [#250](https://github.com/softistx/nxgt-krepo/pull/250) [`a532490`](https://github.com/softistx/nxgt-krepo/commit/a532490c89e008572840e11ec4e1350b69056fd6) Thanks [@SteveGT96](https://github.com/SteveGT96)! - The server dependencies move to their latest patch and minor releases: Ktor 3.6.0, the MongoDB driver 5.13.0, Kafka clients 4.4.0, the RabbitMQ client 5.37.0, Hibernate Validator 9.1.5 and KotlinPoet 2.4.0. Lettuce stays at 7.7 and kotlinx.serialization at the toolchain's 1.11.0.

- [#250](https://github.com/softistx/nxgt-krepo/pull/250) [`a532490`](https://github.com/softistx/nxgt-krepo/commit/a532490c89e008572840e11ec4e1350b69056fd6) Thanks [@SteveGT96](https://github.com/SteveGT96)! - kotlin-reflect moves to 2.4.20, matching the Kotlin stdlib the toolchain ships, and classgraph (classpath scanning in stx-jpa) moves from 4.8 to 6.0.0, a major release of that dependency that needs JDK 25, so a consumer that calls classgraph 4 APIs directly has to move too.

- [#262](https://github.com/softistx/nxgt-krepo/pull/262) [`e7a0481`](https://github.com/softistx/nxgt-krepo/commit/e7a0481579ce8626699bd8091dda570c4741fc9f) Thanks [@SteveGT96](https://github.com/SteveGT96)! - `cors()` now honours `originPatterns` (a glob such as `https://*.example.com`) and refuses an origin written without its `scheme://`; `ContainerService.describe()` resolves the service before saying where it came from; the `stx.telemetry.version` KDoc no longer promises a jar-manifest fallback.
