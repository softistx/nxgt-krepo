Every remaining interactive part takes `style: Style = Style`, applied last, and `SwipeActions` reaches screen readers and keyboards (#288, 3/3). New API: `style` on `PinBar`, `MentionChip`, `FileChip`, `Rating`, `FilterBar`, `ReactionBar`, `LinkPreview`, `ExpandableText`, `CodeBlock`, `ReplyPreview`, `PersonCard`, `Gallery`, `LoadMoreButton`, `ScrollToTop`, `RefreshBox`, `HelpTip`, `Tooltip`, `HoverCard`, `ContextMenu`, `Drawer` and `SwipeActions`; `SwipeActions(customActions: List<CustomAccessibilityAction> = emptyList())`, whose labels are the caller's (set only when the list is not empty); `StxStyles.tile` (0.99 press scale of a clickable `Gallery` image). `PinBar`, `LinkPreview` and `PersonCard` reuse `StxStyles.card`, `MentionChip` and `FileChip` reuse `StxStyles.chip`. Parameter placement: `style` comes after the existing parameters and just before a trailing lambda (`LinkPreview(leading)`, `PersonCard(action)`, `Tooltip`/`HoverCard`/`ContextMenu`/`RefreshBox(content)`, `SwipeActions(content)`, `Drawer(content)`), so `Tooltip("x") { … }` and the like still compile. The one positional case that breaks: `Drawer(open, onDismiss, modifier, gesturesEnabled, drawer, content)` must now name `content` (`Drawer(open, onDismiss, modifier, gesturesEnabled, drawer) { … }` or `content = { … }`), since `style` sits between them; `SwipeActions` likewise puts `style` after `background`. Hosts with a single control forward `style` to it (`FilterBar`, `ReactionBar`, `Rating`, `ExpandableText`, `CodeBlock`, `ReplyPreview`, `LoadMoreButton`, `ScrollToTop`, `HelpTip`); hosts with several controls take none (`SelectionBar`, `Lightbox`, `FabMenu`, `OverflowBar`, `StepFooter`, `NavigationSuite`, `AppBar`). `Menu`, `Sheet` and `ConfirmDialog` take no `style`: M3 draws their popup in its own window and a probe showed `style` does not reach it. On the wrappers (`Tooltip`, `HoverCard`, `ContextMenu`, `Drawer`, `SwipeActions`, `RefreshBox`) there is no default look and the style state has no input behind it: only static properties and `disabled` apply; `pressed`, `hovered` and `focused` never fire. A caller's `style` is never dropped silently any more: without a click handler it still applies, and only the press look and the role go. Visible default changes:

- `PinBar` with `onClick` presses to 0.99 (before: no scale); `LinkPreview` and `PersonCard` with `onClick` are unchanged.
- `MentionChip` and `FileChip` (with `onClick`) press to 0.97 (before: no scale). A `FileChip` without `onClick` does not scale.
- A clickable `Gallery` tile presses to 0.99 and is announced as a button (before: a bare click with no role and no press).
- `DataTable`: the caller's `style` and `headerStyle` now also dress rows and headers that are not clickable (before: they were ignored without `onRowClick` or `onSortChange`).
- `Card` with `onClick` is announced as a button (before: no role).
- `SectionHeader`, the name of a `PricingCard` and the title of a `DangerZone` are announced as headings (before: plain text).
- `SwipeActions` with `customActions` lists them in the row's actions menu (before: the swipe was the only way in).
