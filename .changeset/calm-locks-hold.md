A lease or lock renewal that throws is reported and retried, and the guarded block is cancelled with a `LeaseLostException` once no renewal has succeeded within the duration (the same for `RedisLock`'s watchdog), `CorsPolicy.validate` refuses an origin written without its `scheme://` for Spring as well as Ktor, a cancelled `KeyedMutex` caller or `RedisLock` holder still gives its place back, `RedisStream.history(count)` returns the last entries, and `RedisCache.putAll` writes as one batch.
